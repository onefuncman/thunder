$size[14]{$u{Claims on the Map}}

Show personal claims and village claims as colored tints on the map window and the corner minimap.

$size[12]{$u{How to use it}}
* Open the map window and use the two claim buttons in the top-left icon column (below the heightmap toggle): one for personal claims, one for village claims.
* The same toggles control the corner minimap.
* Both settings persist between sessions, and both actions can be given hotkeys in Options -> Keybindings -> Map window (along with the provinces toggle).

$size[12]{$u{How it works}}
* Claims are drawn from your local map cache, so they appear on any grid you have ever explored - not just what is currently loaded.
* The claim state is as fresh as your last visit: a claim extended or removed since you were last there will show its old shape until you pass by again.
* Colors match the in-world claim display (personal and village each have their own color, provinces are per-realm).
* Overlay transparency is shared with the provinces overlay: hold $b{Shift} and scroll the mouse wheel over the provinces button to adjust it.
