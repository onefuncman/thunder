$size[14]{$u{Placement Snap (Q/E/W/S)}}

While viewing a placement preview for lifted, built or stockpiled objects, press a snap key to place it flush against an existing object or tile edge.

$size[12]{$u{Keys}}
$font[Monospaced,11]{ Q   Snap left}
$font[Monospaced,11]{ E   Snap right}
$font[Monospaced,11]{ W   Snap up}
$font[Monospaced,11]{ S   Snap down}

$size[12]{$u{How it behaves}}
* Cursor $u{on} an object: the placer is pushed flush against that side of the object. The direction key picks which side.
* Cursor $u{near} an object: the placer slides in that direction and stops flush against the first object in its path.
* Cursor on empty ground: the placer aligns with that edge of the tile it is in.

Note: This feature is a work in progress and may struggle with rotated placements.

$size[12]{$u{Tips}}
* Point at the $u{base} of tall objects (cupboards etc.) - the snap targets the ground footprint, not the top.
* The snap direction works according to your camera perspective. Up is towards the top of your screen.
* Repeat the key along a row to build tight lines and grids quickly.
* You might need to ensure focus is on the game world, this can be done by rotating your camera slightly
* Be careful not to move the object after pressing a snap key, as your placegrid fidelity will be set to coninuous.
