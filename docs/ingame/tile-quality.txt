$size[14]{$u{Tile Quality Tracker}}

Thunder remembers the best quality you have ever seen on each tile, automatically, while you play.

$size[12]{$u{What gets recorded}}
* Mining: stone and ore quality per cave wall tile, including strange crystals, petrified shells, quarryartz and cat's gold.
* Digging: dirt and clay quality per tile.
* Water: quality of water filled from freshwater tiles, and saltwater from ocean tiles.

A single tile can hold several kinds at once (e.g. stone + crystal from the same wall).

$size[12]{$u{How to use it}}
* Just mine, dig or fill containers as normal - qualities are captured from the items that appear.
* Open the map window and press the $col[32,245,255]{Q} button to open the Tile Quality window.
* Search for a kind (e.g. a specific stone) to find your best recorded spots; selecting a result navigates the map to it.
* Enable the overlay to tint recorded tiles directly on the map.

$size[12]{$u{Notes}}
* Data is stored locally per map grid, alongside your map cache. Nothing is shared or uploaded.
* Only the highest observed quality per tile and kind is kept.
* You must be present when the item appears - qualities cannot be recorded from areas you have not worked.
